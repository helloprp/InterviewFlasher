Welcome to Yahshua Outsourcing Worldwide Inc.

Thank you for your interest in joining our team. This comprehensive, guided interview will walk you through a series of questions about your background and the role you're applying for.

Each question will appear on screen — please read it aloud yourself, then take your time and answer naturally.

Data Privacy & Consent

In accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), YAHSHUA Outsourcing Worldwide, Inc. and The ABBA Initiative, OPC collect and process the personal information and responses you provide during this interview solely for recruitment and candidate evaluation purposes. Your data will be kept confidential, stored securely, and accessed only by authorized hiring personnel. It will not be shared with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.

This interview will also ask you to briefly introduce yourself, including a short personal and family background and details about your current employment status and reasons for leaving past jobs. This information is collected solely for hiring evaluation purposes.

Before you begin

A couple of quick notes on how this will go:

1. Please read each question aloud yourself before answering. Each question will appear on screen — just read it out loud, then take your time to respond.

2. You'll have 30 seconds to gather your thoughts. A short timer will appear after each question so you can take a moment to organize your answer — but if you're ready to answer right away, that's perfectly fine too.

One last thing before we begin

You'll notice this interview covers quite a few questions — that's by design. Answering these well now means you're less likely to need another full round of questions before moving further in our process, so this is genuinely worth the time you put into it.

Take a breath, get comfortable, and just be yourself. There's no need to rush — clear, honest answers matter far more than perfectly polished ones.

Give it your best shot — we're rooting for you!
